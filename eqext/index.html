eqext Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

eqext ("the app") is an audio equalizer for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, store remotely, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account system, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not send any data over the internet.

Information processed on your device

  • Audio: the app applies Android's built-in audio effects to the playback of music and video apps. Audio is processed in real time by the Android system and is never recorded, stored or transmitted. The app does not use the microphone.
  • Connected audio device names: to switch equalizer profiles automatically, the app reads the name of the current audio output (for example the name of your Bluetooth headphones). This name is stored only on your device, alongside your settings.
  • Player app names: the app shows which player is being equalized (for example "YouTube Music"). This is not stored or shared.
  • Settings and presets: your equalizer settings and saved presets are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or use "Reset all settings".

Sharing presets

If you choose to share a preset, the app opens Android's share sheet with a text code that contains only the preset's name and equalizer values. You decide where it is sent.

Permissions

  • Notifications / foreground service: to keep the equalizer running in the background and show its on/off control.
  • Run at startup: to restart the equalizer after your phone reboots.
  • Modify audio settings: to apply audio effects.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
